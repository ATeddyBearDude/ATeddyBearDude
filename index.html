Sources
Plane/Gaussian sheets are unidirectional (Huygens J+M pair). Plane-wave amplitude is the E-field amplitude of the launched wave. Ellipticity 0° = linear, 45° = circular. Changing λ rebuilds the grid (dx tracks the shortest wavelength).
Media (regions)
Regions apply in order (later overrides earlier), sampled at cell centers. Types: dielectric (εr, μr, σ), PEC (perfect conductor), Drude (dispersive metal: ε(ω) = 1 − ωp²/(ω² + iγω), via ADE).

Physics notes

Classical core. The solver integrates the two time-domain Maxwell curl equations for the full 3-D vector fields E and H — no scalar, paraxial or 2-D reduction. For propagation in vacuum and linear media this is exact classical electrodynamics, and QED reduces to it in the classical/coherent limit; adding quantum formalism to the propagation engine would only reduce accuracy. Nothing in this simulator is quantum. The Hertzian-dipole source is the classical limit of an oscillating emitter (the same radiation pattern QED predicts for the coherent limit of dipole transitions).

Numerics. Yee staggered grid, leapfrog in time, CPML absorbing boundaries, semi-implicit conductivity, Drude dispersion via an auxiliary differential equation. Stability: c·dt ≤ dx/√3 (enforced). Full derivations in METHODS.md.
